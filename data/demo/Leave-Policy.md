# SaiTech Leave Policy

Synthetic company document · Owner: People Operations · Version 1.0 · Effective 1 January 2026

## Annual leave

Full-time SaiTech employees receive 24 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at two days per completed month of service. Part-time employees receive a prorated allowance. Contractors follow their individual agreements and do not receive this employee allowance.

## Requesting leave

Submit planned annual leave in the People Portal at least seven calendar days before the first day of absence. Your manager approves or declines the request within three working days. For absences of five or more working days, include a handover plan and identify a backup contact.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 12 working days of paid sick leave each calendar year. Notify your manager before the start of the workday when practical. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than three consecutive working days. Do not include medical details in public team channels.

## Carryover

Up to five unused annual leave days may be carried into the following year. Carried days must be used by 31 March. Sick leave does not carry forward. Exceptions require written approval from People Operations.

## Emergency leave

For emergencies, notify your manager as soon as reasonably possible. People Operations can approve up to three working days of emergency leave for immediate family circumstances. Emergency leave is separate from the annual allowance.
