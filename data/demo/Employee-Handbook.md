# SaiTech Employee Handbook

Synthetic company document · Owner: People Operations · Version 1.0 · Effective 1 January 2026

## About SaiTech

SaiTech is a fictional software company building collaboration tools for engineering teams. This handbook and all associated policies are synthetic portfolio data, not advice or actual employment terms.

## Working hours

The standard work week is Monday to Friday, 40 hours in total. Teams maintain collaboration hours from 10:00 to 15:00 India Standard Time. Employees agree a schedule with their manager and record planned absences in the People Portal.

## Onboarding

New employees complete security training within five working days. Their manager assigns an onboarding buddy and creates a 30-day learning plan. People Operations verifies emergency contact details during the first week. Equipment requests go to IT through the Service Desk.

## Equal opportunity and conduct

SaiTech expects respectful communication and prohibits harassment and discrimination. Employees may report concerns to people@saitech.example or their manager. People Operations acknowledges reports within two working days and protects information on a need-to-know basis.

## Hiring principles

Each open role requires a written business justification, an approved headcount allocation, and a consistent interview rubric. Hiring decisions must be based on demonstrated skills. The hiring manager records feedback before the decision meeting. Referral candidates follow the same assessment process.

## Policy ownership

People Operations maintains employment policies. Security owns the Security Policy and Incident Response Policy. Questions about a policy should be directed to its named owner. Where this handbook summarizes a topic, consult the dedicated policy for operational details.
