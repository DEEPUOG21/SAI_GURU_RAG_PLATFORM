# SaiTech Expense Policy

Synthetic company document · Owner: Finance · Version 1.0 · Effective 1 January 2026

## Submitting claims

Submit expense claims through the Finance Portal within 30 calendar days of the purchase. Attach an itemized receipt, a business purpose, and the relevant cost center. Your manager approves the claim before Finance reviews it. Approved claims are reimbursed in the next monthly payroll cycle.

## Approval limits

Expenses up to INR 5,000 require manager approval. Expenses above INR 5,000 require department-head approval before purchase. International travel always requires advance approval from the department head and Finance, regardless of the amount.

## Travel and meals

Book economy-class flights for business travel. Domestic hotel reimbursement is capped at INR 6,000 per night before taxes. Business meals are reimbursed up to INR 1,000 per person per day, excluding alcohol. Exceptions must be documented and approved before booking.

## Home internet

Eligible remote employees may claim up to INR 1,500 per month for home internet. Attach the bill and identify the month covered. The unused allowance cannot be carried forward or exchanged for cash.

## Non-reimbursable expenses

Personal purchases, fines, alcohol, and unapproved subscriptions are not reimbursable. Finance returns incomplete claims with a reason. Resubmit corrected claims within ten working days of the return notice.
