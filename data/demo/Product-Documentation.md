# SaiTech Atlas Product Documentation

Synthetic company document · Owner: Product Engineering · Version 1.0 · Effective 1 January 2026

## Product overview

Atlas is SaiTech's fictional project collaboration product. A workspace contains projects, tasks, and shared notes. Workspace admins manage membership and access. Project owners define milestones and assign tasks to workspace members.

## Workspace roles

Atlas supports Owner, Editor, and Viewer roles. Owners manage workspace settings and membership. Editors create and modify project content. Viewers can read content and download exports but cannot modify records. These product roles are distinct from Sai Guru's ADMIN, MANAGER, and EMPLOYEE roles.

## Task workflow

Tasks move through Backlog, In Progress, Review, and Done. Each task has a title, owner, due date, and project. A task cannot enter Review until it has an owner. Closing a milestone does not automatically close its unfinished tasks.

## Data export

Workspace owners can request a JSON export from Settings, then Data Export. The export includes projects, tasks, and notes. Download links expire after 24 hours. Exports do not include passwords, authentication tokens, or audit-log entries.

## Support and retention

Contact support@saitech.example with the workspace identifier and request ID. Do not include sensitive customer data in the ticket. Deleted tasks remain recoverable by a workspace owner for 30 days before permanent removal.
