# SaiTech Security Policy

Synthetic company document · Owner: Security · Version 1.0 · Effective 1 January 2026

## Account security

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for company email, source control, cloud consoles, and the People Portal. Store credentials in the approved password manager. Never share passwords or API keys in chat, documents, tickets, or source code. Use a unique password of at least 14 characters for systems that require passwords.

## Data classification

SaiTech classifies information as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Customer personal data is Confidential. Production secrets are Restricted. Share Confidential and Restricted data only with explicitly authorized people. Do not upload company data to unapproved AI services.

## Device controls

Company laptops must enable full-disk encryption, automatic screen locking after five minutes, and endpoint protection. Install critical security updates within 48 hours of approval by IT. Report a lost or stolen device immediately through the Security Hotline.

## Access reviews

Managers review access to sensitive systems every quarter. Access is removed on the employee's final working day, or immediately when Security identifies a compromise. Service accounts must have a named owner and the minimum permissions needed for their task.

## Reporting suspicious activity

Report suspicious messages, accidental disclosure, or unusual account activity to security@saitech.example. Do not investigate a suspected compromise yourself. Follow the Incident Response Policy for escalation and evidence preservation.
