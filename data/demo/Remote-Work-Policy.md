# SaiTech Remote Work Policy

Synthetic company document · Owner: People Operations · Version 1.0 · Effective 1 January 2026

## Eligibility and schedule

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Teams publish their shared office days by the last Friday of each month. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from People Operations and the department head.

## Collaboration

Remote employees remain reachable during collaboration hours from 10:00 to 15:00 India Standard Time. Keep calendars updated and use asynchronous updates when a meeting is unnecessary. Managers assess outcomes and delivery commitments, not online presence indicators.

## Secure remote access

Use a company-managed laptop and the corporate VPN for internal systems. Public Wi-Fi requires the VPN. Confidential conversations must take place in a private location. Do not allow family members or other people to use company devices.

## Equipment and connectivity

SaiTech provides a laptop and one external monitor. Employees may claim up to INR 1,500 per month for home internet with a valid bill under the Expense Policy. IT support is available through the Service Desk during working hours.

## Location changes

Request approval at least 15 calendar days before working from a different country. Approval is required before travel because employment, data handling, and customer obligations may restrict cross-border work.
